Frequently Asked Questions

NABH accreditation me kitna time lagta hai?
Normally, Entry Level NABH me 3-6 mahine aur Full NABH me 9-12 mahine ka samay lag sakta hai, depending on hospital readiness.
Ayushman empanelment ke liye basic requirements kya hain?
Minimum bed criteria, fire NOC, Bio-Medical Waste authorization, aur basic infrastructure ka hona zaroori hai. Details ke liye humse sampark karein.
GIC tie-up ke kya fayde hain?
Insurance tie-ups se aapke hospital me patient footfall badhta hai aur cashless treatment facility ki wajah se patients ko convenience milti hai.
